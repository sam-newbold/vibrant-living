Your questions answered

Which days is this special available?

The Midweek Reset Escape for Two is available Monday to Thursday only. Weekend bookings are charged at the regular rate of NZ$169 per person.

Do we have to book at the same time?

Yes — to take advantage of the couples midweek rate, both massages must be booked together in the same session. Adela will attend to both of you during your visit.

Why does this need to be a direct booking?

The midweek special rate is available exclusively for direct bookings through our website or booking system. Third-party platforms charge commission fees that make the discounted rate unsustainable.

What should we wear?

You'll be professionally draped throughout — only the area being worked on is uncovered at any time. Arrive in comfortable clothing and leave the rest to us.

Can we choose different massage styles?

Yes. While this is priced as a holistic relaxation massage, you can discuss your individual preferences with Adela when you arrive. Each massage is personalised to the person receiving it.
