Questions

Your questions answered

Will this facial suit my skin type?

Yes — all facials at Vibrant Living are tailored to your skin on the day. Adela will assess your skin before beginning and adjust products and techniques accordingly.

How should I prepare?

Arrive with a clean face if possible, or allow extra time for cleansing on arrival. Avoid sun exposure and harsh exfoliants for 24 hours prior to your treatment.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Please let us know at the time of booking. We use certified organic and natural skincare products, and Adela will select the gentlest options for reactive skin types.

What products are used?

We use certified organic and natural New Zealand skincare products throughout all facial treatments. Ingredient lists are available on request.
