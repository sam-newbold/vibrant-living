Your questions answered

Do I need to believe in energy healing?

No belief is required — just an open and curious mind. Many clients come to healing sessions out of simple curiosity and find the experience deeply relaxing and insightful.

What should I expect in a session?

You'll remain fully clothed, lying comfortably on a treatment table. Sessions are gentle and non-invasive. Adela may use light touch, sound, or guided breath depending on the modality.

How many sessions are recommended?

This varies by individual and intention. Some people find a single session profoundly clarifying; others choose a series. Adela can guide you at your first appointment.

Is this appropriate for emotional difficulty?

Yes. Adela is a qualified practitioner experienced in holding safe space for emotional processing. All sessions are completely confidential.
