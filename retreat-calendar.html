Nourishing stays from 2 to 13 nights

Vibrant Living offers flexible wellness retreat stays from 2 to 13 nights. Choose from scheduled group retreats or create a personalised wellness package on dates that suit you. All retreats are facilitated by Adela — physiotherapist, yoga teacher, and retreat facilitator.

Each retreat is thoughtfully crafted to nurture body, mind, and spirit — combining daily yoga and meditation, organic home-cooked meals, spa treatments, and time in the tranquil two-acre gardens of Hanmer Springs.

A typical day

Sample daily schedule

Each retreat day is thoughtfully structured to balance nourishment, movement, healing, and rest. Here's a glimpse of what a typical retreat day looks like.

7:45am
Ginger Lemon Detox Drink

Start the day with a cleansing, warming detox drink to gently awaken your body.

8:00am
Yoga & Meditation

Guided morning yoga and meditation practice with Adela — for all levels.

9:30am
Healthy Breakfast

Home-cooked organic breakfast, including fresh eggs from our own chickens.

11:00am
Spa Treatment or Session

Your choice of massage, facial, healing session, or other spa treatment.

1:00pm
Free Time / Lunch

Unstructured time to rest, explore the gardens, or enjoy lunch at your leisure.

Afternoon
Rest & Exploration

Visit the Hanmer Springs Thermal Pools, walk the scenic trails, or simply rest in the gardens.

5:30pm
Restorative Yoga / Meditation / Cacao Ceremony

Evening practice to wind down, reflect, and reconnect — rotating each day.

6:45pm
Home-cooked Organic Dinner

Plant-based, nourishing dinner prepared with local and organic ingredients.

7:30pm
Optional Inspirational Film

A curated documentary or film to inspire and deepen your retreat experience.

9:00pm
Journaling / Reading / Rest

Quiet time to integrate the day — journal, read, or simply rest and restore.
