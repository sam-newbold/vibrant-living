Questions

Your questions answered

When does this special run?

The Easter Renewal Experience is available during the Easter period only. Availability is limited, so we recommend booking as early as possible to secure your preferred time.

What type of massage is included?

This is a personalised full-body holistic massage — Adela adapts the pressure, technique, and focus areas to suit what your body needs on the day. It's suitable for most people, including those new to massage.

What products are used?

Adela uses premium certified organic aromatherapy oils and products throughout. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please mention this when booking or on arrival.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable, loose clothing to your appointment. You'll be professionally draped throughout the treatment — only the area being worked on is uncovered at any time.

Can I combine this with accommodation?

Yes — staying at Vibrant Living and adding a spa treatment is one of our most popular combinations. Contact us directly to discuss a stay and spa package for Easter.
