Your questions answered

Will this treatment suit my skin type?

Yes — this treatment is suitable for most skin types. If you have very sensitive skin or a specific condition, please let us know at the time of booking.

How should I prepare?

No special preparation needed. Avoid applying heavy body moisturiser before your appointment so the scrub can work most effectively on clean skin.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Please let us know at the time of booking. We use certified organic products throughout, and Adela will adapt the treatment for sensitive or reactive skin.

What products are used?

We use certified organic cacao butter and natural exfoliants throughout. Ingredient lists are available on request.
