# Friends Escape

## Headline
Friends Wellness Spa Escape — Create Lasting Memories with Your Best Friends

## Body copy
Unwind with your closest friends in Vibrant Living's spacious Three Bedroom Villa, surrounded by stunning mountain views. This package is designed for groups of friends who want to relax, reconnect and enjoy personalised spa treatments together in a private, peaceful setting.

The Friends Escape includes accommodation in the Three Bedroom Villa, a spa treatment each, and a personalised yoga class or alternative wellness experience — everything you need for an unforgettable friends' getaway in Hanmer Springs.

For larger groups of 8–10 guests, the package can be extended to include both the Three Bedroom Villa and the Lodge Suite accommodation.

## Listed items / services

### Accommodation
- Three Bedroom Villa (sleeps up to 8 in standard booking; up to 10 with Lodge Suite add-on)
  - 2 queen bedrooms
  - 1 twin room (2 single beds)
  - 2 fold-out couches (for larger groups)
  - Large lounge, full kitchen, laundry
  - Large deck with BBQ
  - Mountain views
  - Heat pump

### Included in the Package
- Accommodation in Three Bedroom Villa
- 60-minute massage or facial (per person) at the Day Spa
- 60-minute personalised yoga class in the yoga room or garden (all equipment provided)
  - Alternative: Cacao Ceremony or Sacred Shaking session

### Optional Add-ons
- Additional spa treatments (massages, facials, pampering packages)
- Extra nights' accommodation
- Organic breakfast add-on
- Private healing or coaching session
