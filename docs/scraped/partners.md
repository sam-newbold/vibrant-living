Local Favourites — Our Recommended Hanmer Springs Businesses

## Body copy
We've gathered together a collection of popular activities, popular restaurants, handy services and a variety of shops you can visit while you are in Hanmer Springs. As local experts, we love sharing the best of our village with our guests.

## Listed items / services

### Activities & Attractions
- **Hanmer Springs Hot Pools** — One of Hanmer Springs' most popular attractions for all the family. The famous thermal hot pools are located in the village centre.
- **Hanmer Springs Animal Farm** — Located right next door to Vibrant Living Retreat on Rippingale Road.

### Transport
- **Hanmer Tours & Shuttle** — Shuttle bus services between Christchurch and Hanmer Springs, with airport pickups and drop-offs that can be arranged.

### Shopping
- **Mhor** — A female fashion store geared to 30+ women, located on the corner of Conical Hill Road and Chisholm Crescent.
- **Up The Garden Path** — A wonderful arrangement of kitchen, gift and accessories. The Majuba Gallery is located next door, featuring beautiful jewellery and art.
- **Ula** — Asian fashion, jewellery, gifts and lots more.

### Dining
- **Malabar Restaurant** — A fusion of European and Asian cuisine.
- **No. 31 Restaurant** — A Modern European Restaurant.
