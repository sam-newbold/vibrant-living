What's included in the package time?

The package time covers all treatments listed, plus transition time between them. You'll have everything you need — robes, slippers, and refreshments are all included.

Some flexibility is available within each package. Contact us before booking if you'd like to discuss specific preferences or substitutions.

Just yourself. We provide robes, slippers, towels, and all products. Wear comfortable clothing to your appointment.

Yes — additional treatments can often be added. Please contact us when booking to discuss availability and pricing.
