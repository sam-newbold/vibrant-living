Things to Do in Hanmer Springs

A mountain village with more than you might expect — hot pools, alpine trails, adrenaline, and deep rest.

Your complete local guide

More than just hot pools

Hanmer Springs sits in a broad alpine basin in the Lewis Pass foothills, roughly 90 minutes north of Christchurch. It is, at once, one of New Zealand's most beloved weekend escapes and one of its best-kept secrets. Beyond the famous thermal pools lies a whole landscape worth exploring — ancient beech forest, mountain bike trails, river canyons, and a village unhurried enough that you might actually feel yourself slow down. Here is our guide to making the most of it.

The essential stop

Hanmer Springs Thermal Pools & Spa

The thermal complex at the heart of the village is the reason most people first discover Hanmer Springs — and rightly so. Geothermally heated pools range from pleasantly warm to genuinely hot, and a visit here after a day on the trails is one of life's simple pleasures. There are outdoor and indoor pools, private thermal pools, a hydroslide complex for families, and a café on site.

It gets busier on weekends and during school holidays, so an early morning or weekday visit rewards you with a far more tranquil experience. If you are staying at Vibrant Living, the pools are less than five minutes by car or a pleasant fifteen-minute walk through the village.

  • Adults from NZ$22 · children from NZ$14
  • Private pools bookable in advance

On foot

Walks & hikes for every level

The Hanmer Forest Park wraps around the township and offers a network of trails from gentle afternoon strolls to full-day ridge walks with sweeping mountain views.

1–1.5 hrs return · Easy–Moderate

Conical Hill Loop

The town's most-loved short walk climbs through fragrant exotic forest to a summit lookout with panoramic views over the entire basin. Accessible for most fitness levels and genuinely rewarding.

2–3 hrs return · Easy

Dog Stream Waterfall

A peaceful valley walk through beech forest to a beautiful waterfall — ideal for families and those looking for a gentle immersion in native bush without the elevation gain.

5–7 hrs return · Strenuous

Mt Isobel

The big day out. The ridge walk to Mt Isobel rewards the effort with arguably the finest 360° alpine view in the Hanmer region — worth every step for seasoned walkers.

Trail maps available at the i-SITE visitor centre on Amuri Avenue or via the DOC website.

Two wheels

Mountain biking in the forest

Hanmer Springs has developed into one of Canterbury's most popular mountain biking destinations. The forest park network includes trails for all levels — from wide gravel tracks perfect for beginners to technical singletrack that keeps experienced riders coming back.

Bikes can be hired in the village, or if you'd prefer something a little more unconventional, we offer fat bike hire at Vibrant Living. Fat bikes handle the gravel and rougher terrain with ease and are a genuinely fun way to explore the area at a relaxed pace.

For the thrill-seekers

Adventure & adrenaline

The Waiau River gorge and surrounding landscape provide the backdrop for some seriously exciting activities — all within a short drive of the village.

Thrillseekers Canyon

Jet Boating

Blast through the narrow Waiau Gorge at speed — 360° spins, cliff faces, and pure exhilaration. One of the most memorable 30 minutes you'll spend in the South Island.

Thrillseekers Canyon

Bungy & Swing

The Pipeline Bungy drops 35 metres above the Waiau River. Not for the faint-hearted — but the views from the top and the rush on the way down are something else entirely.

Thrillseekers Canyon

White Water Rafting

Grade 3–5 rafting on the Waiau — suitable for complete beginners through to those after a serious challenge. Full wetsuits and safety briefing provided.

Book directly with Thrillseekers Canyon — located on the Waiau Gorge Road, 5 minutes from the village.

The village

Eating, drinking & wandering

The village of Hanmer Springs is small enough to walk end to end in twenty minutes, but it has more than enough to keep you occupied between activities. A handful of good cafés, some excellent spots for lunch, a few boutique shops, and a general atmosphere that invites you to slow down.

Amuri Avenue is the main street and home to most of the eateries, the i-SITE visitor centre, and the thermal pools entrance. Grab a coffee, browse the local shops, or simply sit in the sun — Hanmer does unhurried exceptionally well.

If you are staying at Vibrant Living, we offer home-cooked organic breakfasts for accommodation guests, and Adela can point you toward her favourite local spots for lunch and dinner.

Getting here

How to reach Hanmer Springs

From Christchurch

By car

Approximately 90 minutes via State Highway 7 through the Waimakariri Gorge — one of the most scenic drives on the South Island. The road is well-sealed and suitable year-round.

From Nelson

Via Lewis Pass

Around 2.5 hours via the Lewis Pass (State Highway 7) — a dramatic mountain crossing through native beech forest. Stop at the natural hot pools at Maruia Springs on the way.

Public transport

InterCity Bus

InterCity runs scheduled coaches from Christchurch to Hanmer Springs. The journey takes around 2 hours. Check the InterCity website for current timetables and bookings.
