Good to know

Your questions answered

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2:00pm and check-out is by 10:00am. Early check-in or late check-out may be available on request — just let us know when booking.

Is parking available?

Yes — free off-street parking is available on-site at Vibrant Living. The property is located at 88 Rippingale Road, Hanmer Springs.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is included in our B&B rate. Room-only rates do not include breakfast, but organic breakfast can be added for NZ$40 per person when booking.

Can I add a spa treatment to my stay?

Absolutely — day spa treatments can be booked separately via our online booking system. Our Stay & Spa Packages offer the best value when combining accommodation and treatments.
