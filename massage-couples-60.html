Questions

Your questions answered

What should I wear?

You'll be fully draped with a towel or sheet throughout your massage. We provide robes and slippers — simply wear comfortable clothing to your appointment.

How should I prepare?

Arrive 10 minutes early to settle in. Avoid a heavy meal in the hour before your treatment, and let us know of any injuries, allergies, or areas to avoid when you arrive.

Can I request a specific pressure?

Absolutely. Adela always checks in before and during your session — communication is welcome at any point. Your comfort is the priority.

What products are used?

We use certified organic aromatherapy oils and professional-grade products throughout. Please let us know of any skin sensitivities at the time of booking.
